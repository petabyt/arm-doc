4.6 Audio
4.6.1 Multichannel Audio Serial Port (MCASP)
This section contains the integration details for the McASP module on this device. For Further information, see
the Multichannel Audio Serial Port (MCASP) section of the Peripherals chapter
4.6.1.1 MCASP Unsupported Features
The following features are not supported on this family of devices:
•
Muting output (AMUTE)
•
Muting input (AMUTEIN)
•
Instances may not support all pin options. See device specific datasheet for details on which McASP instance
support which pins
