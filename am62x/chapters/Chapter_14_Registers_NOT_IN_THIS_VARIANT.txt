Chapter 14 - Registers
======================

This chapter is NOT present in the "noRegisters" variant of the AM62x TRM
(SPRUIV7B).  Register descriptions were removed from this edition; the
body text refers the reader to TI's online register supplements, e.g.
  https://www.ti.com/lit/pdf/registers  (AM62x register reference)

The printed table of contents still lists chapter 14 subsections
(14.1 ... 14.8.x) with page numbers from the full TRM (with registers);
those page numbers do not apply to this document.
